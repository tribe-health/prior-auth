## Purpose

Specify the observable contract for this runtime capability: open cited sources through an authorized adaptive preview. These requirements preserve verified scope and honest delivery evidence across the application.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Open cited sources through an authorized adaptive preview — outcome 1

The system SHALL satisfy the following outcome: The preview uses the audited byte service, presents provenance and keyboard/focus controls, and respects bounded fetch behavior.

#### Scenario: An authorized citation opens its source at a known page/date

- **WHEN** an authorized citation opens its source at a known page/date
- **THEN** The preview uses the audited byte service, presents provenance and keyboard/focus controls, and respects bounded fetch behavior.

### Requirement: Open cited sources through an authorized adaptive preview — outcome 2

The system SHALL satisfy the following outcome: Access is denied or aborted; bytes/handles/object URLs are released and old content never appears in a new scope.

#### Scenario: A forbidden document is requested or logout occurs during fetch/render

- **WHEN** A forbidden document is requested or logout occurs during fetch/render
- **THEN** Access is denied or aborted; bytes/handles/object URLs are released and old content never appears in a new scope.

### Requirement: Open cited sources through an authorized adaptive preview — outcome 3

The system SHALL satisfy the following outcome: Dialog/sheet adapts without duplicating editors; focus returns correctly, navigation remains reachable, safe areas and 44px touch targets hold.

#### Scenario: The viewport crosses mobile/desktop thresholds or reduced motion changes

- **WHEN** the viewport crosses mobile/desktop thresholds or reduced motion changes
- **THEN** Dialog/sheet adapts without duplicating editors; focus returns correctly, navigation remains reachable, safe areas and 44px touch targets hold.
