Gap, not met, not documented

The queue distinguishes three states that most tools collapse into one. Conflating them is how a letter ends up asserting something the chart does not support.

Met

Criterion satisfied and traceable to a dated source document in the packet.

Not met

Evidence exists and falls short of the policy threshold. This is a clinical fact and the letter may address it directly.

Not documented

No source found. Never rendered as “not met” and never asserted in a letter — it is a records gap, and it gets a task, not a sentence.
