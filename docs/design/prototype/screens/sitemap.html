Nineteen screens.
One gate that matters.

The product is an entry point, a linear case pipeline, and a flat settings area. Every screen is reachable by every role — what changes is what you can do once there.

Tier 1 · Entry Chooses the persona. In production this is federated sign-in; in the prototype it is a picker that also previews everything the selected person can open.
EntryROOT Sign in screens/login.html Persona picker with a live site-map preview. No password. Surgeon and staff land on the case queue; an administrator lands on the admin console. Leads to Case dashboard · Admin console · any screen in the preview list
Tier 2 · Case pipeline Ten steps in order. Steps 01–06 assemble and decide; step 06 is the gate; steps 07–10 exist only because a surgeon affirmed it. The prototype lets you view 07–10 early, with a banner saying the gate has not been affirmed.
Tier 3 · Settings & administration Flat, not sequential — reachable any time from the global rail. These screens configure where cases come from and who may do what to them.

Who can do what

Access is not the variable — every role opens every screen. The variable is capability, and the two clinical ones are deliberately scarce.

An administrator configures the system but cannot attest to medical necessity. That split is a clinical-safety boundary, not an org-chart convenience.

The gate is the structure

Steps 01–05 gather evidence and rank pathways but decide nothing. Step 06 is where the surgeon confirms policy, section, pathway and operative plan. Steps 07–10 are downstream consequences of that one decision — which is why the sitemap has a waist rather than a flat list.

Two entries into the pipeline

The dashboard links straight to whichever step a case is actually blocked on, so a coordinator opening a records-incomplete case lands on the intake checklist while a case awaiting a decision lands on pathway comparison. The pipeline is ordered, but you rarely walk it from the top.
