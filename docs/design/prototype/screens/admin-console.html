The boundary an administrator cannot cross

An administrator can create accounts, assign roles, and connect EMR systems — but cannot affirm a surgeon gate or apply a physician's signature, and cannot grant themselves that ability. Promoting an account to Surgeon requires a recorded credential: license number, NPI, and a signature the physician uploads themselves.

Access & retention

Every PHI read is logged with user, case, and timestamp. Sessions expire after 30 minutes idle. Audit records are retained six years and cannot be deleted from this console.

AI-use disclosure

Letters are drafted with model assistance and attested by a physician. That disclosure is recorded per case, so the attestation chain is evidenced rather than asserted if a payer or regulator asks.
