Receipt-verification notice

Sent after each material submission. It names the exact manifest rather than asking whether records arrived, so a later dispute has something specific to contradict.

Sent 2026-09-02

Subject: Confirmation of Records Submitted — Kaminski, Renata — PA-2026-0431

On 2 September 2026, our office transmitted the records listed below in support of the above authorization request:

[ 11-item document manifest · attached ]

Please notify our office promptly if any listed item was not received, is incomplete or illegible, or is not available to the physician who will conduct the utilization review. If we receive no notice identifying a missing or unavailable item, our office will rely upon this transmission record in proceeding with the authorization and peer-review process.

If a subsequent adverse determination or peer-review discussion asserts that a listed record was not available for review, we will compare that assertion with our transmission documentation and this notice. Material discrepancies in the handling or availability of submitted clinical information may be referred, when appropriate, to the governmental or regulatory entity having jurisdiction over the health plan or utilization-review process.

Thank you,
Office of Kevin B. James, MD

Why the wording is careful

The notice states what our office will rely on, not what the carrier is deemed to have received. Silence creates a contemporaneous record and an opportunity to object — it does not create a legal presumption, and this office does not assert one.

One critical item disputed

Physical therapy records are policy-critical under §4.1(c) and the reviewer stated they were not in the file. Transmission evidence contradicts that.
