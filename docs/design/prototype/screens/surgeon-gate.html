Regulatory route · resolved before drafting

Fully insured Texas PPO. Medical-necessity appeal runs through the plan's internal process, then to independent review under the Texas Department of Insurance IRO program. Regulatory complaint jurisdiction is TDI, not DOL/EBSA.

The letter's accountability paragraph will be written to that jurisdiction specifically. A self-funded plan under the same insurer's brand would route to DOL/EBSA instead.

What affirming does not do

It does not append to an existing draft. On affirmation the letter is regenerated in full from the confirmed evidence model — policy, section, pathway, operative plan and every cited source — because addenda make a medical-necessity letter longer and less persuasive.

It also does not apply your signature. That happens once, after you have read the finished letter, at step 07.
