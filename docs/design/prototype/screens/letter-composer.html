Policy applied: SURG-2026-014 rev. 4, §4.1 (effective 2026-07-01)

I am requesting authorization for right L4–L5 decompression with instrumented posterior fusion for Ms. Kaminski under §4.1 of Medical Policy SURG-2026-014 rev. 4. The adequate decompression of her severe right L4–L5 foraminal stenosis requires resection of more than fifty percent of the right L4–L5 facet joint, and §4.1(d) treats a planned facetectomy of that extent as establishing the segmental instability that makes concurrent fusion medically necessary. Each element of §4.1 is addressed below in the policy's own order.

§4.1(a) — Duration and failure of conservative management

Ms. Kaminski has had right L4 dermatomal pain for fourteen months, first documented at consultation on 19 November 2025, with standing tolerance under ten minutes and walking tolerance of two blocks. CLIN-0977 · p.2 She underwent two right L4–L5 transforaminal epidural steroid injections: the first on 9 April 2026 produced seventy percent relief lasting eleven days, the second on 21 May 2026 produced fifty percent relief lasting six days. PAIN-0884 · p.1 PAIN-0912 · p.1 Non-steroidal and gabapentinoid trials are documented as exhausted prior to referral. PAIN-0912 · p.2

§4.1(b) — Imaging correlation with examination findings

CT myelography on 22 July 2026 demonstrates severe right L4–L5 foraminal stenosis with deformation of the exiting nerve root. RAD-1341 · p.2 This correlates with the examination: diminished right patellar reflex at 1+ against 2+ on the left, and a positive right femoral stretch test reproducing anterior thigh pain — both referable specifically to the L4 root. CLIN-0977 · p.3

I want to address the earlier MRI directly rather than leave the reviewer to find it. The MRI of 8 January 2026 reported no definite nerve root impingement. RAD-1180 · p.1 The studies are six months apart, and the interval radiographs document progression at this level.

§4.1(c) — Supervised physical therapy

Ms. Kaminski completed nine supervised physical therapy sessions between 11 February and 18 March 2026 — five weeks. PT-0219 · p.2 Her Oswestry Disability Index moved from 48% to 44% across that course, and she was discharged for non-progression rather than for completion of a planned program. PT-0219 · p.3 §4.1(c) is satisfied by the second clause — therapy discontinued for non-progression — rather than by the six-week duration. I note the distinction explicitly so that the element is not read as a shortfall against the duration threshold.

§4.1(d) — Instability

The decompression required to relieve this patient's foraminal stenosis cannot be accomplished by foraminotomy alone. Adequate exposure of the right L4 root in the foramen requires resection of more than fifty percent of the right L4–L5 facet joint. CLIN-1142 · p.2 §4.1(d) provides that a planned decompression requiring facet resection of that extent satisfies this element without reference to a radiographic threshold. Independently, flexion-extension radiographs of 22 July 2026 demonstrate six millimetres of anterior translation of L4 on L5, above the four-millimetre threshold in the same subsection. RAD-1342 · p.1 I cite the translation as corroboration; the request rests on the planned resection.

§4.1(e) — Preoperative optimization, and a disclosure request

Serum cotinine and HbA1c results are not currently in the record, and I am not representing that either has been established. Both are being obtained and will be submitted on receipt. If authorization of any component is denied on this basis, I request that the determination identify the specific criterion and policy version applied, per §6.4 of the same policy, together with the specialty of the reviewing physician. Given that this request turns on whether a facetectomy of the described extent destabilises a lumbar segment, review by a physician who performs lumbar spinal surgery would be substantively material.

Why this construct, at this level

The request is for a single level. No adjacent segment is being instrumented, and no pelvic fixation is planned, which is why no bone-density assessment accompanies this submission. The interbody device is included because a facetectomy of the described extent removes the posterior tension band on that side; posterior instrumentation alone would leave the segment reliant on a compromised facet complex for rotational stability. Ms. Kaminski's axial back pain is part of the clinical picture but is not offered as the basis for coverage.

Appeal, external review, and regulatory jurisdiction

Ms. Kaminski has been provided information concerning the applicable internal appeal process, the independent review organization process administered under the Texas Department of Insurance, her right to request the specific criteria applied to her request, and the process for filing a regulatory complaint. Should an adverse determination result from the application of criteria unrelated to the submitted indication, from review by a physician without relevant specialty qualification, from failure to disclose the operative criteria on request, or from other material deficiency in the utilization review process, the patient and I reserve the right to request review by the governmental entity with jurisdiction over this health plan. This plan is a fully insured Texas product, and that entity is the Texas Department of Insurance.
