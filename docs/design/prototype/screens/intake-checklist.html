Add documents to a category

Uploads are classified on arrival and attached to the category they satisfy. A document that satisfies nothing the policy asks for is held, not filed.

Two absences block the primary pathway

Nicotine status and HbA1c are conditions of the fusion criterion in §4.2(c). Until a source document exists, a fusion pathway cannot be ranked as strong — and the letter will not claim optimization that no record supports.
