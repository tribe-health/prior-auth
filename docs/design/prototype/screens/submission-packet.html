What happens after submission

If approved

The authorization number, the authorized CPT list, and the validity window attach to the case. The scheduler is blocked from booking beyond the expiry date, and any override is recorded against the person who made it.

The biller receives the authorized codes and is alerted if the operative codes diverge from them.

If denied

The denial is classified as fix-and-resubmit or appeal within the first read. A records problem does not consume a peer-to-peer slot.

An appeal is drafted with the §4.1 criteria crosswalk already in it, and the request for criteria disclosure made in the original letter becomes the record that the disclosure was asked for before the determination.

Accountability record

Once transmitted, the case can answer the second question the packet cannot: can we prove the payer had this evidence when they evaluated it?
