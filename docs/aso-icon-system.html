Icon System · v1.0 · August 2026

Two counter-sweeps

One mark, six deliveries. The device is lifted directly from the wordmark: a warm sweep crossing a cool one. Doubled and opposed, it reads as a motion segment — the anatomy the practice actually treats — without becoming an illustration of one.

Primary containers

App stores and operating systems mask corners themselves. Ship a full-bleed square with no baked rounding and no transparency; the shapes below are previews of how each platform will render it.

Primary · dark ground
The default. Ink ground holds the ember at any size.
Alternate · bone ground
Light-theme docks, print, letterhead marks.
Knockout · ember ground
Restricted. Notification badges and single-color stamps only.

Scale test

The gap between the two sweeps is what carries recognition at small sizes. It is also the first thing to close up — which is why the favicon variant is drawn at 116% rather than reusing the app icon.

Maskable & safe area

Android adaptive icons crop to an arbitrary shape and may cut anything outside the centre 66% of the canvas. The maskable variant scales the mark to 70% so no part of either sweep can be clipped, whatever mask the launcher applies.

Export note

Rasterize from aso-icon-dark.svg at 1024px, not from a smaller PNG. The sweeps have long shallow curves; upscaling a 512px raster produces visible stair-stepping along the tapers that is not obvious until it hits a Retina dock.

Rules

DoDon't
Keep the ember sweep on the bottom. The order is fixed. Flip, mirror, or rotate the mark beyond its built-in −13°.
Ship full-bleed squares and let the OS mask. Bake rounded corners into the source SVG.
Use the maskable variant for anything Android. Reuse the app icon as a favicon — it closes up at 16px.
Use aso-icon-mono.svg where a single color is required. Recolor either sweep outside the token set.
Pair the icon with the full lockup on first-run and letterhead. Use the icon as a replacement for the wordmark in documents.
Open item

This mark is original work derived from the sweep in the practice's existing logo. Before it goes on an app store listing, have whoever holds the practice's trademark position confirm they are comfortable with a derived icon — and check that no orthopedic group in the DFW market is already using a similar double-arc device.
