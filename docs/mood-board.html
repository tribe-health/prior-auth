Imagery direction
Anatomical models in shallow depth of field, clinical rooms with natural light, hands and instruments — never stock-photo smiles. Warm neutrals against cool equipment. The existing site already does this; keep it.
Motion direction
A single easing curve echoing the logo sweep. Nothing bounces. State changes cross-fade over 160ms. The affirmation gate does not animate — it waits.
