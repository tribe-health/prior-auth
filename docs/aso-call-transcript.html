Appendix A · Glossary

TermMeaning in context
Foraminal stenosisNarrowing of the opening where a nerve root exits the spine. Graded mild / moderate / severe by the radiologist. Moderate or severe is the threshold that makes fusion authorizable.
Iatrogenic instabilityInstability created by the surgery itself. Resecting more than half of both facet joints to reach the foramen destabilises the segment, which is what indicates fusion.
Lateral recessThe region between the central canal and the foramen. Decompression must extend here and into the foramen — not stay central — to address foraminal stenosis.
EHLExtensor hallucis longus. Great-toe extensor, supplied by L5. Weakness here is the motor finding that correlates to L5 root involvement.
4/5 strengthMovement against resistance but weaker than normal. The documented grade that establishes neurologic deficit without being complete paralysis.
ACDFAnterior cervical discectomy and fusion.
Lumbar 360 fusionFusion achieved from both anterior and posterior approaches.
BUCAIndustry shorthand: Blue Cross, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Aetna.
eviCoreEvernorth utilization management vendor. Administers Cigna spine and interventional pain, and some Aetna lines.
ODGOfficial Disability Guidelines. Adopted by Texas DWC as the workers' compensation treatment standard.
No Surprises ActFederal law creating an independent dispute resolution process for out-of-network payment disputes.
Federal IDRIndependent Dispute Resolution — the arbitration mechanism under the No Surprises Act.
Peer-to-peerCall between the treating surgeon and the payer's reviewing physician, usually preceding or following an adverse determination.
