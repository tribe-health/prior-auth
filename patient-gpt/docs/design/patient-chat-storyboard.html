PatientGPT

Prior authorization, worked by chat. It looks and behaves like the chat app you already use. Patients sit where projects would, and each case is a conversation that knows the payer’s policy, the chart and what is still missing. Everything in it is synthetic.

Or just ask

With no patient selected, every chart is in scope. When the assistant cannot tell which case you mean, it asks, then carries your request into the case you pick. Every answer ends with what to ask next.

  • What’s waiting on me?
  • Who is missing a lab result?
  • Draft the letter
  • Where does Brennan stand?
  • Anyone with a foot drop?
