Three icon concepts

Each concept is shown where it will live: the iOS and Android home screens, a browser tab at favicon size, the wordmark in light and dark, and the app's own sidebar. All three use the practice palette only: navy for the field, ember as the single accent, paper white and the ink grey.

Concept A

Spine in conversation

The practice's two sweeps, kept exactly as drawn, inside a speech bubble. It says what the product is in one glance: the ASO mark, talked to. Closest to the existing brand; the sweeps need the bubble to be large, so it is the softest of the three at 16 px.

Concept B

P monogram

A geometric P whose bowl carries an ember speech tail: PatientGPT as a letterform. One shape, two colours, no gradients. It is the strongest at tiny sizes and the easiest to set next to the ASO logo without competing with it.

Files: ./b-p-monogram/ · icon, mark, mark-dark, favicon, wordmark-light, wordmark-dark

Concept C

Stage track

Three rounded segments, the current one in ember with a tail: a stylised spine column that is also the case-state track the app shows on every case row. It is the only concept that comes from the product's own interface rather than the practice's mark, and it reads as 'progress you can talk to'.

Before production

  • The wordmark text is live Playfair Display in the SVG files. Convert it to outlines before shipping so it renders without the font installed.
  • Icons are drawn on a 512 canvas; export the 1024 master, then the platform sets, from icon.svg. The tile is full-bleed navy; iOS and Android apply their own masks.
  • favicon.svg is the simplified mark on a rounded navy tile for 16 and 32 px; use mark.svg / mark-dark.svg at 24 px and above.
  • Ember on white uses the text ember (#A85417); ember on navy or dark uses the brighter mark ember. Do not swap them.
